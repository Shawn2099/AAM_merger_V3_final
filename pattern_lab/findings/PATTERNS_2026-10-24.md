# Patterns found in the real-run corpus (read-only analysis)

Date: 2026-10-24
Source of truth for data: **a copy** of `data/aam_merger.db` taken with SQLite's
backup API while the source was opened `mode=ro`.

- Lab DB: `pattern_lab/db/aam_merger_lab.db` (integrity_check: ok)
- Source DB at copy time: `f103eb60d3617e66ed7733fb9fff8f860e5e16585a0db1723c0f72a0ad2a8298`
- Rendered pages: `pattern_lab/renders/` (PyMuPDF raster, read as images — the
  PDF text layer was deliberately **not** trusted)
- Helper scripts used for the counts: `pattern_lab/scripts/`

Nothing outside `pattern_lab/` was written. No API/VLM call was made. No app
code was changed.

Corpus shape: 200 documents (80 PO / 59 DN / 61 SI, all `valid`, none
archived), 480 line items, 57 PO Sets — 12 merged, 24 pending, 18 quarantined,
3 mismatched.

---

## P1 — The dominant failure is page-fragment ingestion, not matching

**Observation.** Multi-page vendor documents reach the pipeline as one file per
page (or per 1–2 pages). Every page repeats the same document number, so the
same physical document appears as several `documents` rows.

**Evidence (DB).** 16 sets hold a repeated `(doc_type, document number)` pair:

| what repeats | sets | effect |
|---|---|---|
| PO with a **single** distinct PO key, 2–8 rows | 3, 7, 12, 13, 14, 15, 17, 18, 22, 23, 32, 33, 34, 35 | `multiple_po_documents` → `pending` |
| SI with the same SI number on 2 rows | 7, 15 | `duplicate_document_number` → `quarantined` |

All 14 multi-PO sets have **exactly one distinct `po_no_normalized`**. There is
not one genuinely multi-PO set in the corpus.

**Verified by eye.** `SIV-ARS-25-7969-pages-3.pdf` = "Page 1 (3)" of PO
50119501, lines 1–3. `-pages-4.pdf` = "Page 2 (3)" of the *same* PO, lines 4–6.
`SIV-DTS-25-456-pages-1.pdf` and `-pages-2.pdf` are both invoice
SIV-DTS-25-456 with the same Delivery Note GDN-DTS-25-493, lines 1–12 and
13–18. The reconciler sums all rows of a set, so *matching* is unaffected — only
the two guards misread the repeats.

**Limits.**
- The filename ordinal is **not** the printed page number: `-pages-1.pdf` can
  hold printed pages 1–2, `-pages-3.pdf` can hold printed page 1. Do not derive
  page positions from filenames.
- Set 15's SI is missing line 11 on the paper (10 → 12), so a fragment merge
  must not "repair" numbering.
- Set 23 is two *different* packets (`SIV-DTS-25-538`, `SIV-DTS-25-548`) sharing
  PO key 4500047182, with overlapping line keys 20–70. Any future stitching by
  PO key alone would double-count here.

## P2 — Line-number convention follows the **buyer**, and the buyer is identifiable

| Buyer (issuer of the PO) | Printed line numbering | Verified on |
|---|---|---|
| ADES (Standard Purchase Order) | `1, 2, 3 …` | doc139, doc104 |
| Arabian Drilling | `10, 20, 30 …` | doc45, doc50 |
| Northern Offshore | `10, 20, 30 …` | doc151 |
| Noble Drilling | **two-level** Line + Rel ("1/1", "2/1", "2/2") | doc163 |
| Valaris / Ensco | `1-1` (printed) | doc169 |
| ARO (Saudi Aramco Rowan) | `1, 2, 3 …` | doc8 |

The supplier does *not* determine the convention — RAK Energy supplies ADES,
Northern, Noble and Valaris packets with four different numbering styles.

**New, usable signal.** `SIV-RAK-*` filenames carry the buyer token
(`-Ades-`, `-Northern-`, `-Noble-`, `-Ensco-`), and the token matched the buyer
company printed on every PO page I opened. The naming scheme is
`SIV-<SUPPLIER3>-<YY>-<SEQ>[-<BUYER>]-<ordinal>.pdf`, consistent across all 200
files (4 supplier families: ARS, DTS, RAK, RHO).

**Limits.** None of this is a matching input — it is only evidence about *which*
transform to expect, and it is absent for ARS/DTS/RHO packets (they have no
buyer token in the filename).

## P3 — Duplicate line numbers on a PO can be legitimate (two-level numbering)

**Observation.** Noble POs print two stacked numbers per row (Line + Rel). The
extraction flattens them to the Line number, so the same key legitimately
appears twice.

**Evidence (DB).** doc163 (PO 300101295): line `2` twice with quantities 4 and
12, identical description; line `21` twice with 4 and 1. Nine of the 480 rows
carry the printed `N-1` form; the rest are flat digits.

**Why it matters.** "Two PO rows with the same line number" is not by itself a
data error, and summing them (today's behaviour) is the only sane reading. It is
also a trap for any future uniqueness assumption or duplicate-detection rule.

## P4 — "PO read but has no line items" = the cover page was supplied, not an extraction failure

**Evidence.** 10 PO documents have zero line items across 9 sets (9, 17, 18, 28,
32, 34, 35, 56, 57). Verified by eye:

- doc200 (Raas → NOMAC) page 1 of 13: header, bank details, terms; the item
  table header prints at the very bottom of the page with **no rows**.
- doc163 (RAK → Noble) page 1 of 6: cover page, no items; items begin on page 2.
- doc45 (DTS → Arabian Drilling) page 1 of 8: one item only.

So for these layouts page 1 always yields 0 items. The system's wording ("pages
with the items may be missing from the input") is correct; what is missing is a
way to tell the operator *how many* pages should exist.

## P5 — Printed "`<digits> , 0`" is literal document text, not OCR noise

**Observation.** 15 `po_no_raw` values look like `'193165 , 0'`, `'22613 , 0'`,
`'13060 , 0'`.

**Verified by eye.** The ADES "Standard Purchase Order" prints a field labelled
**`PO , Rev #`** whose value is literally `13060 , 0`. The model copied the cell
faithfully; the normaliser's digit extraction recovers `13060`.

**Consequences.**
- The revision is captured and then discarded. A revised PO (same number, new
  Rev) keeps the same grouping key — intended per the SPEC, but now confirmed as
  a real artefact, not a parse bug.
- Other layouts print the revision separately ("Purchase Order : 90139566
  Rev : 1" → raw `90139566`), so the shape of `po_no_raw` is layout-specific.

## P6 — Printed page totals are unusable as a completeness signal

Three behaviours coexist in the same corpus:

| Document | Printed | Reality |
|---|---|---|
| doc104 (ADES) | "1 of 6" | the item table and the PO total both end on page 1; pages 2–6 are T&C |
| doc45 (Arabian Drilling) | "Page 1 of 8" | only page 1 supplied, 7 pages genuinely missing |
| doc163 (Noble) | "Page 1 (6)" | 6 PO pages + 7 attachment pages in one 13-page PDF |
| doc169 (Valaris) | "Page 1" | no total printed at all |
| doc8 (ARO) | "Page 1 (3)" | page 3 genuinely missing |

The extracted `raw_extraction_json.page_count` is equally inconsistent: 7 for a
7-page file (doc139), 1 for the 1-page file doc104 that *prints* "of 6". Do not
build a completeness guard on either number.

## P7 — Attachment pages are already separated, and that is working

4 files report two components: the PO section and `SKIP` pages
(doc145 pages 2, doc160 pages 4–10, doc163 pages 7–13, doc166 pages 5–13). The
model is correctly not treating T&C/attachment pages as line items. No action;
worth preserving if the prompt is ever touched.

## P8 — Description overflow becomes a phantom 0-quantity PO line

**Evidence.** 4 of the 5 numberless rows are PO rows with `quantity = 0` whose
description is a wrapped continuation of the item text
(doc46 "AD-76 NON STOCK\nHammer: Sledge 7 Lbs NON SPARK RIG", doc77
"QTN-DTS-25-2547\nRack Squat for Gym", doc83/doc89 "Non-absorbing PVC compound
retained with aluminum …"). The fifth numberless row is a real DN row
(doc22, set 7, 50 000, DUCT TAPE), and it is the row that quarantined set 7's
line matching.

They do not change a verdict today (`non_positive_quantity`/description keying
absorb them), but they inflate line counts and they are the only thing that ever
triggers the description fallback path.

## P9 — `vendor_name` identifies the buyer on POs and the supplier on DN/SI

42 of 57 sets contain more than one spelling; 33 distinct strings fold down to 6
real companies plus noise (`RAAS`, `raas`, `M/S RAAS OILFIELD SERVICES AND
SUPP.`). On PO pages the field carries the **issuer/buyer** (ADES, Noble,
Valaris, Northern, NOMAC, Arabian Drilling); on DN/SI it carries the supplier.

This independently confirms the decision not to match on vendor name — and it
explains *why* it looked plausible: within one set the values differ by document
type, not by vendor.

## P10 — Description is not a safe matching signal either

For the 134 vendor rows that already match a PO line **by number**, description
similarity is: min 0, p10 33, median 67, p90 97, max 100 — only 40 of 134 pairs
reach 85. Vendor pages add suffixes such as "Line Item - 1", Arabic text and
bracketed part codes (`[PROTO - J710B]`), and PO descriptions are truncated.

Consistently, `po_sets.description_match_count` is **0 on all 57 sets**: the
description fallback has never linked a row in this corpus.

## P11 — Two small structural hazards for PO-number identity

1. **Cross-references to other POs are printed on PO pages.**
   - doc52: "REPLACEMENT OF DELETED PR#10022850 / PO# 4500026535 AS PER THE
     ATTACHED MAIL"
   - doc163: "[HOLGER - Previously Purchased on PO-300069041_14-05-25]"
   - doc151: "Quotation Ref : QTN-RAK-25-4845", "Req Number 2101005296"
   A reader that takes "the first PO-like number on the page" would scatter the
   set. The current extraction kept the right key every time I checked.
2. **The grouping key is digits-only**, so a customer prefix is invisible:
   `TOGA-PO-25-26-163` → `2526163`, `ARM-PO-25-26-029` → `2526029`,
   `LPO26/2360` → `262360`. No collision exists in this corpus (no PO key is
   shared by two sets), but two different customers issuing the same serial in
   the same year would collide. PO keys observed by length: 5 (24), 6 (26), 7
   (15), 8 (42), 9 (6), 10 (66), 12 (3), 15 (15).

## P12 — Unattached documents are few and explainable

4 documents have no PO Set: 3 with no PO number at all, 2 flagged
`po_reference_ambiguous` (documents 2 DN + 1 DN + 2 SI). Nothing systemic; the
Unclassified path is holding them as designed.

## What this does **not** show

- No merge in the corpus is provably wrong on quantities: replaying the product's
  own `compare_po_set_lines` over the copy reproduces `FULL_MATCH` for exactly
  the 12 merged sets.
- Letting the reconciler see fragments does **not** by itself unlock merges: of
  the 14 fragment-blocked sets, only 2 have no line-level objection (7, 32);
  10 fail on numbering, 2 are genuine partial deliveries. The value of fixing
  P1 is a truthful status and reason, not 14 new merges.
- Replaying the four candidate line transforms reproduces the known x10 sets and
  additionally flags **14 and 22** (x10, single-line packets) plus a partial
  match in 23 — cases not listed in `LINE_NUMBER_FALLBACK_PLAN.md` §7. Set 15
  shows why all-or-nothing is right: SI x10 works, but PO key 110 does not
  exist, so a per-key partial mapping would fabricate a match.

## Suggested next decisions (not implemented)

1. Decide the fragment policy (stitch by identical document number within a
   set, or keep the guard and improve the reason text). P1 says the current
   reason misleads the operator.
2. Add 14 and 22 to the line-mapping evidence list; re-check 23 for the
   two-packets-one-key hazard.
3. Do not use printed page totals or `page_count` for completeness. If
   completeness matters, it needs its own source of truth.
4. Treat duplicate line numbers on Noble-style POs as normal (P3).
